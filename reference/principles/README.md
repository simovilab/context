# Principles

Foundational design and architectural principles that govern SIMOVI research and development. These documents establish the intellectual and engineering foundations for all SIMOVI projects.

- [`architecture.md`](architecture.md) — High-level architecture diagrams for Databús and Infobús, showing component relationships and data flows.
- [`system-design.md`](system-design.md) — SIMOVI's ten core system-level design principles, organized into business, data, application, and technology categories. Derived from published research and industry frameworks (MDIP, MACH, TransitOPS).
- [`data.md`](data.md) — FAIR Data Principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) as adopted by SIMOVI for research data management and open science.
- [`methods.md`](methods.md) — Methods for vehicle location data ingestion, including recommended MQTT-based telemetry approach and alternative REST polling patterns.
- [`tech-stack.md`](tech-stack.md) — Full technology stack inventory: Python, Django, Celery, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, TimescaleDB, Redis, RabbitMQ, NanoMQ, FastMCP, Apache Jena Fuseki, Nuxt, and more.
- [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md) — SIMOVI development roadmap and planned milestones.
- [`software.md`](software.md) — Software engineering principles and practices adopted by SIMOVI.
- [`trl.md`](trl.md) — Technology Readiness Level (TRL) framework as applied to SIMOVI projects.
